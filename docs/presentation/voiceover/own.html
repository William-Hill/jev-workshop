READYFOLLOWS YOUR VOICE
The highlight follows your voice (browser speech recognition; Chrome sends the audio to Google for this).
Without it, the highlight moves at a steady reading pace.
Space: record, after a 3-second countdown.
Space or Enter while recording: stop and keep the take.
P: rehearse. With the camera on, a rehearsal is also a take.
Esc: stop and discard.
E: edit the script. It is saved in this browser.
The take ends by itself 1.2 s after the last word.
Read at your own pace: the video is timed to your take later.
